# Endpoint classification: structural reduction and strict novelty audit

Date: November 21, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles).

## Decision

The previous first-damping-derivative theorem survives adversarial review and now has a stronger algebraic interpretation. The two polynomial tests are equivalent to having only central unobserved directions inside the Jordan algebra generated by the measurements. This proves exact endpoint neutrality at finite damping, whenever the compared flows converge to positive-definite interpolants; convergence is established locally for small targets.

The novelty assessment becomes more conservative. The resulting Jordan structural class, invariant products, conservation method, and Bregman uniqueness argument have substantial classical antecedents. Retain a narrow candidate theorem about the finite recognition test and its equivalence to fixed-quadratic-weight endpoint sensitivity. Do not present this as a new Jordan classification, a new metric, or an established feature-learning mechanism. Priority of the precise recognition/optimization bridge remains unverified after this bounded primary-source audit.

## Model and exact quantifiers

Let S be a real linear subspace of Sym(n), with the Frobenius inner product. Set X0=sI, s>0. The product-space flow of the specified balanced two-factor, full-representation model is

\[
\dot X=-(I+\lambda T_X)^{-1}G_B(X),\qquad
T_X(U)=(L_X+R_X)^{-1}U,
\]

where G_B is the gradient of a fixed positive quadratic measurement loss and belongs to S. B is held fixed throughout each run. The same linear measurements and target are used when weights are compared.

Write J=Jordan(S,I) for the unital matrix Jordan algebra generated by S, with product U circ V=(UV+VU)/2, and N_J=J intersect S-perp. The following conditions are equivalent:

1. For every Z,U in S,

   \[
   [Z,[Z,U]]\in S,\qquad [Z^2,[Z,U]]\in S.
   \]

2. Every member of N_J commutes with every element of J.
3. S contains the full traceless part of each non-scalar simple Jordan factor of J, together with a subspace of the center. Equivalently, S is an associator ideal of its generated J. The commuting central part need not itself be closed under multiplication.
4. The normal inverse-Lyapunov one-form on (sI+S) intersect SPD is exact; its primitive is one half of the normal projection of log X.
5. For all sufficiently small targets, the first right derivative at lambda=0 of the interpolating endpoint is independent of all fixed SPD quadratic weights.

The prior report established 1, 4, and 5. This round proves the structural bridge to 2 and 3 and derives the finite-damping extension. Uniform small-target convergence is asserted for each bounded nonnegative damping interval and each compact SPD weight family. No uniformity over arbitrarily ill-conditioned weights is claimed.

Failure of condition 1 yields an actual fixed-weight witness: a cubic target-amplitude term when Lie-triple closure fails, or a quartic term when only the second test fails. This is an existential statement over small targets and weight pairs. It is not a statement about every target, every pair, or a single arbitrary prescribed positive damping value.

## The new bridge and why it matters

The first step is a positivity lemma: the normal square defect P_N(Z^2) commutes with every generator in S. Two independent derivations reached this fact, one using differentiated commutators and the other using the positive operator ad_U^2.

Extending degree-two centrality to the whole generated algebra requires a real argument. The proof decomposes S into irreducible modules under k=[S,S]. Distinct nontrivial modules commute and have zero mutual Jordan product. On each module S_i, the product has the form

\[
Z\circ U=m_i(Z,U)+\langle Z,U\rangle a_i,
\]

with central a_i. The identity ad_Z^2 U=4[Z^2 circ U-Z circ(Z circ U)], together with Frobenius trace associativity, forces multiplication by a_i to preserve S_i. This multiplication is scalar, and a_i produces a central idempotent. Thus R e_i plus S_i is a simple Jordan factor. The remaining generators are central. This closes the previously missing passage from local polynomial identities to the entire generated algebra.

The complete proof is in [ALGEBRA_REDUCTION.md](ALGEBRA_REDUCTION.md). The independent review is in [REFEREE_AUDIT.md](REFEREE_AUDIT.md). The review found one minor wording correction in the old prose: the velocity, residual, and weight derivative decay exponentially; the trajectory itself approaches its target. No archived record was altered.

## Finite damping and an explicit endpoint

For passing spaces, the flow stays in J. For every N in N_J, centrality gives the exact conserved quantity

\[
\left\langle N,X+\frac{\lambda}{2}\log X\right\rangle.
\]

Consequently every SPD interpolating limit is the unique minimizer

\[
X_\infty=\operatorname*{argmin}_{X\succ0:\mathcal A X=y}
\left\{\frac12\|X-sI\|_F^2+
\frac\lambda2\operatorname{tr}\big[X(\log X-(\log s)I)-X+sI\big]\right\}.
\]

This is an ambient SPD Bregman projection, not merely minimization within J. The reason is that the conserved normal coordinates and functional calculus in J give the full constraint optimality condition. Strict convexity then gives uniqueness.

Thus a passing space cannot develop a hidden second-order or higher-order damping dependence on the loss weights while the flows still converge to SPD interpolants. The old first-order cancellation is the derivative of this exact law. This result does not establish global convergence for arbitrary targets. The proof also gives sufficiency for SPD X0 in J, with the corresponding Bregman divergence, but does not classify arbitrary nonisotropic initializations.

The details and convergence qualifications are in [FINITEDAMPING_AUDIT.md](FINITEDAMPING_AUDIT.md). The conservation-to-KKT argument is a standard consequence once the structural bridge is known; it is not counted as a separate conceptual invention.

## Primary-source novelty audit

The audit searched equivalent algebraic descriptions and tried explicit theorem substitutions, rather than relying on an exact keyword search. Important newly examined originals are:

- [Benito, Draper, and Elduque, Journal of Algebra 219 (1999), 234-254](https://investigacion.unirioja.es/documentos/5bbc680bb750603269e8017c/f/5ddc084152922232560fc774.pdf), especially sections 3.7 and 4.2-4.3: invariant products on simple Lie triple systems and their relationship with Jordan traceless modules. This is a strong antecedent for the module reconstruction portion of our argument.
- [Bresar, Cabrera, Fosner, and Villena, Studia Mathematica 169 (2005), 207-228](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/90340), definition of Lie triple/associator ideals and Theorem 1.1. This identifies the classical ideal-theoretic language into which the classified spaces fall.
- [Barnum and Hilgert, Spectral Properties of Convex Bodies, Appendix A, Lemma A.1](https://winephysicssong.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/BarnumHilgertSpectralPropertiesOfConvexBodiesPreprintVersion2.pdf): irreducibility of the traceless component of a simple Euclidean Jordan algebra. Once derivation invariance is known, the structural form is a known representation-theoretic consequence.
- [Marcotte, Gribonval, and Peyre, NeurIPS 2023](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/c7bee9b76be21146fd592fc2b46614d5-Paper-Conference.pdf), Proposition 2.5, and their [ICML 2024 extension](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.12888v1): general conservation-law methods, including non-Euclidean flows, already explain the endpoint-identification strategy.

The complete comparison also covers SLD/BKM metric relations, statistical sufficiency, SLD torsion, Jordan sufficient algebras, universal mirror representations, and current covariance conservation results. Several tempting substitutions fail because they use tangent metric equality rather than normal cross-block equality, assume an algebra from the outset, or allow arbitrary time-dependent losses instead of two fixed quadratic weights. These failed substitutions do not establish novelty; they delimit what the inspected theorems directly imply.

## New minimal validation

Only one new structural case was simulated: J=Sym(2) direct-sum Sym(3) direct-sum R, embedded in Sym(6). S contains both traceless factors and one central measurement coupling all three blocks. It has dimension 8 and two central normal directions inside J. This tests the new multiple-factor, coupled-center finite-damping conclusion rather than repeating the previous star-plane failure or a single traceless block.

At s=1 and lambda=1.7, the two fixed loss weights were the identity and a dense SPD matrix with eigenvalues 0.7, 0.9, ..., 2.1. Each was integrated at two step sizes, for four runs total. An independent two-variable solve of the conserved-coordinate equations predicted the endpoint.

| Check | Result |
|---|---:|
| Endpoint difference between weights, fine step | 8.44e-13 |
| Largest fine-step discrepancy from invariant prediction | 1.21e-12 |
| Largest coarse-to-fine change | 1.75e-11 |
| Largest measurement residual over four runs | 2.11e-14 |
| Minimum sampled trajectory eigenvalue | 0.81707 |
| Ambient optimality residual of predicted endpoint | 3.26e-16 |

The coarse weight gap was 1.33e-11 and dropped by about a factor of 16 on step halving, consistent with the fourth-order integrator. These results support implementation consistency, not originality or a universal theorem. The proof carries the general statement. Reproducible inputs, all four outputs, and source provenance are included in [DATA_DICTIONARY.md](DATA_DICTIONARY.md).

## Research disposition

Retain the exact recognition-and-static-weight equivalence as a modest, sharply scoped mathematical candidate. Its strongest formulation is now clearer, and no fatal proof gap was found. The known Jordan and conservation structure substantially narrows what can be claimed as new. This round supplies no evidence for predictive advantage, generalization, deep nonlinear networks, or a distinct feature-learning mechanism, so it does not reopen the retired empirical routes or justify large training runs.

The next publication-level question is priority and significance of the finite recognition bridge itself. A search that does not locate an identical formula is insufficient to answer that question affirmatively. This archive is a completed exploration record with a proved scoped result and a cautious novelty verdict, rather than an originality certificate.
