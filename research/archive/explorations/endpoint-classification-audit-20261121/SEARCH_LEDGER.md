## Citation chains pursued and limits

1. Brešar et al.2005 identifies the Lie-triple ideals with Jacobson associator ideals and points to Jacobson1968 ChapterVIII. It cites Jacobson p408 Thm17 for the Albert-algebra case. The book was inaccessible, so these are attributed through the2005 paper, not independently verified book claims.
2. The2005 Lemma3.1 invokes Zelmanov1983 *Prime Jordan algebras II*, identity(3), and McCrimmon–Zelmanov1988(12.11). These were located bibliographically but not independently read; no assertion that they prove the candidate generator bridge is made.
3. Barnum–Hilgert LemmaA.1 traces its structural proof to Satake Thm8.5/Lemma8.6 and Faraut–Korányi III.2.1/III.3.1. This round uses the inspected primary lemma, not uninspected book statements.
4. Petz→Jenčová–Petz→Jenčová2012→2026 real/Jordan sufficiency was followed at theorem level. The support condition and explicit Bures counterexample prevent an unsupported reduction to quantum recovery.
5. Nagaoka–Fujiwara cites Amari–Nagaoka's torsion formula and Ohara2004 on dual connections and symmetric cones. The exact book/Ohara theorem was not independently available in this round. Existing torsion is credited; no priority inference is drawn from lack of access.
6. Benito–Draper–Elduque1999 follows Lister1952 on simple LTS, Faulkner1980/1985 on diagrams/invariant products, Laquer1992 on invariant connections, and Neher1985 on Lie/Jordan triple classification. These upstream sources were located bibliographically; their exact full theorems were not independently read. The1999 primary theorem and reconstruction passage, rather than uninspected ancestors, support the report's overlap judgment.

## Honest negative-search interpretation

The search did not locate the same normal-projection finite criterion or static-weight bridge. This is not a proof of absence. In particular, classical Jordan texts, older journal archives, and equivalent formulations of partial integrability may contain a generator lemma under different notation. The team subsequently validated its finite-generator-to-central-normal bridge independently. The report therefore separates that potentially additional recognition lemma from the existing structural classification and the standard conservation/Bregman consequences. It does not infer priority from the absence of an exact keyword match.
