# Matching training loss across learning rates

Every comparison starts from the same seed's 100th pullback update. The GD rates are 0.5, 1 and 2. Training stops at nominal time 1,600 after the switch. We set the following rules before opening any new loss trajectories.

* The primary common training-cross-entropy target is 0.05 nats. Other fixed targets are 0.20, 0.10, 0.075, 0.04, 0.03 and 0.02. We also match each seed to the realized training loss of its rate-2 model at GD update 400. Only training inputs and their labels determine the matched states.
* Start with the first downward crossing under each rate. Report the first actual iterate at or below the target and its overshoot. As the primary comparison, bisect the straight segment of parameter space between the two successive GD iterates to reach the same training loss within numerical tolerance. These interpolated models are points on the piecewise-linear discrete path. Also report the comparison using the two rates' actual first-crossing iterates.
* As a sensitivity check, require a crossing to remain at or below the threshold for 10 further units of nominal time. This corresponds to 20, 10 or 5 additional updates for the tested rates. Match the loss on the same bracketing segment. A target at the chosen step-400 reference uses that exact rate-2 model in the seed-specific comparison; the persistence condition applies to the other two rates. A reference target above the common starting loss is ineligible.
* Give all coverage counts. Restrict a comparison to seeds for which all three GD rates reach the relevant target under that matching rule. Within a seed, fit each region table on the same 8,192 unlabeled inputs and score the same 32,768 held-out inputs and batch tie keys. Reuse the exact 64-per-256 allocation budget. The loss gap D is the region rule's loss minus the confidence rule's loss. Positive D favors the confidence rule.
* Summarize paired changes in D between rates, region wins by rate, crossing overshoot and number of steps to reach the target. For pointwise 95% bootstrap intervals draw complete seed pairs, separately for the original and independent cohorts. Combined intervals give equal weight to the two cohorts when both have full coverage, and otherwise weight individual complete pairs equally. Intervals are exploratory for the secondary loss targets.
