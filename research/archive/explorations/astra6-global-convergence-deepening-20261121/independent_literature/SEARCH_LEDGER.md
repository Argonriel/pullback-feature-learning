## Source integrity interpretation

The sources/*_web_retrieval.json files store the tool-return extraction records (including URL and requested line positions), not complete source PDFs.
SOURCE_MANIFEST.json hashes those local records and the local audit/check files. These hashes verify preservation of exactly what was retained; they do not certify the publisher's original bytes or completeness of a paper. The root package should preserve this distinction.
