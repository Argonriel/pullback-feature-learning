# New-direction screening register

Date: November 21, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This is a bounded literature-first research screen, not an externally preregistered statistical trial. The register records the questions and selection rules; deterministic witness calculations are falsification/reduction checks, not predictive-performance estimates.

The prior archive inventory is the exclusion list. Do not repeat joint asymmetry, unread-feature decoupling, broad learned-geometry feedback, parity/noise-replica/curvature/commutation criteria, inference-time symmetry calibration, budgeted second views, rank certificates, distillation, or stale-metric experiments as new ideas.

Four distinct problem settings are investigated:

1. Homogeneous/deep-linear representation pullback: does finite damping or aggregation of intermediate representations give a new depth-dependent implicit bias beyond known natural-gradient and mirror-descent results?
2. Stochastic minibatch pullback: does joint sampling of metric and gradient create a specific useful or harmful phenomenon not already covered by biased preconditioned SGD, independent-batch corrections, or normalized-gradient reweighting?
3. Representation topology: do input-map folds or near-invertibility create an optimizer-specific predictive condition, rather than the known expressivity restriction of homeomorphic feature maps?
4. Separately sampled geometry as a poisoning/control channel: is there a concrete threat model or defense beyond ordinary robust covariance estimation, metric reweighting, or standard data poisoning?

For each setting, inspect original papers before mathematical or numerical validation. Record exact assumptions and the strongest overlapping result. Reject broad formulations immediately if already covered. A narrower variant may be screened only if it states a different concrete prediction and information model. The absence of an identical paper title is not novelty.

Permitted minimal validation: exact algebraic reductions, finite enumerations, small fixed examples, derivative checks, or reuse of archived states as explicitly retrospective observations. No large network sweep or previously reserved confirmation cohort is used. Preserve all selected and failed construction attempts when there is a search. Distinguish a direction worth further research from a verified innovation.

Keep code and reusable experiment inputs in the repository's fresh `experiments/new_direction_screen_20261121/` directory. Save English reports, calculations, provenance and data in a fresh independent archive. Preserve all historical files and repository junctions. Do not contact anyone.
