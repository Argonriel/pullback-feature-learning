# Validation checklist

## Scope and literature

- Read repository instructions and inventory the prior archive before selecting new settings.
- Read original papers before each corresponding minimal check; distinguish exact overlap from analogous framework or differing assumptions.
- Check recent exact joint-GN scalar results rather than claiming their damping/depth formulas as new.
- Independently audit the fixed-weight endpoint question against mirror-flow, time-warp, and Bures–Wasserstein sources.
- Preserve the failed deterministic tangent gate and all earlier retired criteria. No threshold revision or reserved-cohort reuse.

## Mathematical and numerical checks

- Diagonal full-factor solves agree with the explicit mobility; prefix-coordinate reductions agree with direct solves.
- Full matrix parameter velocity agrees with the reduced Lyapunov-operator flow at the checked state.
- Endpoint comparison uses identical interpolation constraints and identical representation-metric definition; only positive loss weights differ.
- Reduced endpoint residuals are below 1e-11; step refinement is recorded separately from the initial probe.
- Independent original 8-parameter integration reproduces the surviving finite-damping endpoint gaps within 3e-10 after refinement; residuals are below 1e-12.
- Zero damping and an underdetermined diagonal mirror model provide negative controls.
- Factor balance, product symmetry, and positive definiteness are monitored; no claim of an unrestricted rank-boundary theorem.
- Exact binomial summation and conditional-label enumeration validate stochastic statements without Monte Carlo uncertainty.
- Topology witness checks the original core forward map and parameter derivative, plus an independent finite-difference input derivative.
- Geometry-poisoning predictions match direct matrix solves; effects are rejected as routine reductions rather than promoted from their magnitude.
- Independent mathematical response review is included and its analytic assumptions are separated from numerical checks.
- For the fixed two-measurement family, the response review proves the positive first-order contrast and a local infinite-time remainder; this does not establish broad originality or a global damping theorem.
- Initial failed quadrature precision and later 64/128-node agreement are documented with the limit that the initial full-file hash was not captured.

## Preservation and reproducibility

- Keep initial, refined, and independent calculations in separate directories.
- Keep the initial independent parameter source alongside its result hash.
- Package current reusable source, core dependency, instructions, and environment lock.
- Resolve source hashes for result records.
- Verify every file in both immediately previous archives against their manifests: 105 learned-geometry files and 32 structural-condition files.
- Generate and verify a fresh archive manifest. The machine-readable `ARCHIVE_CHECK.json` is the final confirmation of these preservation checks.

No large training run, external message, new population-benefit claim, or verified originality claim is included.
