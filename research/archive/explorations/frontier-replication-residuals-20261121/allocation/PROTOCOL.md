# Prospective two-stage allocation frontier

This is a new, independent experiment. The previously completed allocation study and all its sources/results are immutable. This protocol, runner, calibration/model helper, development-only preflight and complete-fit benchmark are frozen before opening any 241-family training, calibration or evaluation stream. Internal scientific review must release the exact source freeze before execution.

## Frozen grid and training arithmetic

Keep the same two-amplitude synthetic law, four-dimensional representation, 144-parameter residual architecture, sampled-label mean logistic loss, and full-batch PB optimizer. Each fitted procedure uses exactly 4,352 accepted, unique fitting labels. Four fixed allocations are considered:

| Procedure | Training labels | Validation | Calibration labels | PB updates | Rate | Damping |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| train_2304 (baseline) | 2304 | 0 | 2048 | 500 | 36 | 0.9 |
| train_3072 | 3072 | 0 | 1280 | 375 | 48 | 1.2 |
| train_3584 | 3584 | 0 | 768 | 321 | 56 | 1.4 |
| train_3840 | 3840 | 0 | 512 | 300 | 60 | 1.5 |

The previous nominal arithmetic budget remains exactly 207,260,412,000 operations. Each procedure executes floor(budget/C(n)) updates for `C(n)=179045*n+2001144`, with less than one update unused; maximum relative shortfall is 0.30552%. This matches a declared arithmetic model, not elapsed time, hardware instruction count, energy, or all special-function/memory costs. Diagnostic checkpoints at floor(25%,50%,75%,100%) of the schedule are saved and scored on training data only. They never change stopping or selection. All four diagnostic losses and state copies are costed separately from nominal optimizer arithmetic.

No validation allocation is added. This is a prospective choice based on the previous independent study: its validation procedure selected the final update in 15/16 seeds and showed no clear improvement while spending calibration labels. The present larger training pools and shorter schedules do not supply a new positive early-stopping rationale. The design addresses the remaining high-training-label frontier without an additional validation search. Validation labels/forwards are explicitly zero.

The original metric sums full representation Jacobians while loss averages examples. Rate and damping therefore scale as `4*n/256` and `0.1*n/256`, equivalent to normalized metric `J.T@J/n + (0.1/256)I` at rate `4/256`. Preflight checks duplication at factors 2,3,9,12,14,15, normalized-metric equivalence, and independent directional finite differences using only old development examples. No prospective hyperparameter pilot is permitted.

One update executes n standalone representation forwards plus n forward examples inside the analytic Jacobian, 4n Jacobian rows of 144 scalar entries, n loss derivatives, one Jacobian-transpose vector contraction, a 144 by 144 Gram matrix and a general LU solve. There is no separate reverse-mode backward call. Count one addition/subtraction/multiplication/division as one nominal FLOP and a length-k dot product as 2k-1. The two standalone representation blocks cost 272n; the two analytic Jacobian blocks cost 11,152n; scalar logistic derivative costs 5n; Gram costs `144^2*(8n-1)+144`; negation/RHS costs `576n+144*(8n-1)`; LU costs 1,980,264; substitutions cost 41,328; rate scaling/parameter addition costs 288. This sums to C(n). Each update also has 32n tanh, n exp and n logaddexp evaluations, listed separately rather than assigned arbitrary equivalent FLOPs. Pivot comparisons, memory, control/argument scalar overhead and transcendental internals are excluded from C(n).

## Development selection, fixed before outcomes

Run all four procedures on eight paired development seeds, sharing initialization and nested training data within each pair. Training pools consist of independent class-balanced increments 2304,768,512,256, followed by a deterministic shuffle. A procedure sees only its own prefix. Calibration uses a common iid 2,048-example pool with the designated prefix for each procedure; calibration is independent of training. All accepted training data and rejection-proposal counts are retained.

Fit separate positive scalar inverse temperatures for raw logits and full half-turn mean logits using sampled calibration labels. The bounded interval `[exp(-3),exp(3)]` and derivative-bisection implementation are inherited unchanged. Five output arms are retained: raw uncalibrated, raw own temperature, full uncalibrated, full raw-temperature transfer diagnostic, full own temperature. Full output means `(h(x)+h(partner(x)))/2`. Raw/full calibration shares paid raw and partner outputs (actual 2n forwards), while standalone raw/full alternatives cost n/2n. Temperature scalar iterations, derivative/objective evaluations, and label-evaluation counts are recorded. Positive temperatures must preserve classification signs.

After all development models and temperatures are committed, score 65,536 common iid sampled-label evaluation examples per paired seed. **Select exactly one grid member solely by mean sampled-label NLL of raw own-temperature output over the eight paired seeds.** No full-output result, error metric, posterior risk, calibration metric or timing may influence selection. If multiple means are within 1e-12 of the minimum, choose the smallest training n. The sampler internally uses the posterior to generate labels, but development prediction archives contain only x/y and logits; no posterior-based development metric is computed. All development grid results are reported, and the chosen development minimum is explicitly selection-biased.

`SELECTION_COMMIT.json` records the chosen identity, all four selection scores, exact criterion, development score-commit hash and summary hash before any confirmation stream is opened. The selected identity cannot be changed by confirmation, timing, negative results, or additional analyses.

If the baseline wins selection, retain it honestly. The confirmation stage trains and evaluates the baseline once per fresh seed, reports fresh absolute performance, and records selected-minus-baseline differences as zero by construction with **no improvement test or confidence interval**. It does not promote a runner-up or invent an independent duplicate competitor. If a higher-training candidate wins, confirmation evaluates that one candidate and the baseline only.

## Independent confirmation and inference

Run 16 new paired confirmation seeds after the selection commit, with new training, initialization, calibration and evaluation streams. Keep the selected recipe and both baseline/selected temperature-fitting procedures unchanged. Commit all confirmation models, then all temperatures, before opening confirmation evaluation inputs. Each pair has 65,536 common iid inputs and sampled labels; posterior expected risks are used only for final confirmation evaluation, never selection or fitting.

The two primary estimands are selected-minus-baseline expected NLL for raw own-temperature and full own-temperature outputs. Negative favors the selected candidate. For a distinct selected candidate, use 20,000 paired bootstrap resamples of the 16 independent seed clusters and two-sided alpha 0.05/2 per primary contrast. This gives approximate multiplicity-adjusted bootstrap uncertainty, not a finite-sample certificate. Expected error, sampled-label NLL/error, expected Brier and reliability are secondary; secondary contrast intervals are descriptive 95%. An interval crossing zero is inconclusive, not proof of equivalence. The independent confirmation addresses winner's bias from development selection; it does not establish global optimality over untested allocations or external tasks.

All dev/confirmation raw and full unscaled per-item logits, labels, training states, initializations, inputs, checkpoints and calibration outputs are retained. Confirmation archives additionally retain the true posterior for independent risk reconstruction. Five inference arms are physically replayed: raw [256] examples and full [256,256] examples per batch. Uncalibrated diagnostics use fewer fitting labels and are not described as equal-budget calibrated competitors. No interpolation field or selective serving is included.

## Label and computational accounting

The 4,352 label budget is **per fitted procedure**, not the end-to-end cost of discovering a recipe. The development fit-label union is 5,888 per pair (3,840 training and 2,048 calibration), or 47,104 for eight pairs. Development selection additionally consumes 524,288 labeled evaluation items, shared across candidates. All development candidates' fitting, calibration, diagnostic and evaluation work is counted. These development labels/work are not hidden in the final per-procedure budget.

For confirmation, the fitting-label union per pair is max(2304, selected training n)+2048, because alternatives share the nested pools. Only training increments needed by the selected/baseline pair are generated. Confirmation evaluation consumes 1,048,576 additional labeled items, with posterior access separately disclosed. Synthetic class-conditional rejection proposals are generation overhead, recorded independently from accepted fitting labels. Training forward/Jacobian/solve counts, diagnostic forwards, calibration forwards/scalar operations, serving forwards, unique-label unions, and all development search overhead are separate ledger fields. Ordinary concurrent fit elapsed times are descriptive and support no timing comparison.

## Prior art, limitations and reproduction

Temperature scaling is established ([Guo et al., ICML 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html)); this is not a new calibration method. Compute allocation is established broadly ([Hoffmann et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556)); their large-model scaling result does not establish this small PB frontier. Selection bias from optimizing noisy evaluation criteria is established ([Cawley and Talbot, JMLR 2010](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html)); this study uses a separate prospective confirmation rather than treating the winning development mean as unbiased evidence.

Reusable code lives in the new repository directory `experiments/allocation_frontier`; this branch contains protocol/results and a portable frozen source copy. Stages are: preflight, freeze, internal release, development train/calibrate/score, select, confirmation train/calibrate/score, analyze, exclusive timing, accounting/report. Every stage verifies source/protocol and prior commitments. Existing source/results remain untouched. All outcomes, including baseline selection, inconclusive contrasts, deterioration and calibration boundary optima, will be reported in English artifacts; root supplies the overall Chinese summary and research archive.
