- Andreas Maurer and Massimiliano Pontil (2009), [Empirical Bernstein Bounds and Sample Variance Penalization](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~colt2009/papers/012.pdf), Theorem4. - Pascal Massart (1990), [The Tight Constant in the Dvoretzky-Kiefer-Wolfowitz Inequality](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176990746), Annals of Probability18(3),1269-1283. The empirical-law verifier uses the uniform empirical-CDF bound with constant2 and an independent verification sample.

The actual top64 inclusion polynomial, input-law calculations, local enclosures and empirical results are derived or computed within this study. None of the model-performance numbers comes from these references. Known-law and empirical-law certificate families have separate confidence allocations.
