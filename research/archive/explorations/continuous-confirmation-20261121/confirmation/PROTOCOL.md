# Independent confirmation of a confidence allocator with calibrated angles

This protocol is frozen before any new model training. It confirms the previously observed benefit of keeping a first-view confidence mask fixed and replacing its half-turn by a calibrated radius-specific angle. Pullback at 8,192 calibration forwards is primary. GD and PB100→GD400, global-angle and joint-radius policies, the original IID folded gate, and a 65,536-forward sensitivity are fixed controls. No method or rate is retuned.

Two geometry cohorts each contain 16 independently seeded training/initialization pairs. Original rings use train821001–821016/init822001–822016. Ellipses use train821017–821032/init822017–822032, observations x=A z with A=diag(1.5,0.7), where z follows the original noisy-rings law. Schedules within each pair share data and initialization; the two geometry cohorts use distinct draws and are not paired with one another.

The network trains on observed x without whitening. Every selection rule knows the same A and has access to latent z=A^-1x. The valid transformation is A R_alpha A^-1, preserving latent radius and the label law. The ordinary Euclidean rotation of an ellipse is not used or assumed to be valid. The half-turn remains x→−x. The known posterior on latent radius is used only for evaluation; no calibration label is observed.

Primary calibration uses 64 independent radii and 128 angular nodes at each radius, totaling8,192 forwards. Secondary calibration uses512 radii and65,536 forwards, with the primary radii as a prefix. Sixteen radius quantile bins and the exact same minimum-four-radii fallback,64-angle candidate grid, log-loss Jensen curves and argmax tie rule are imported unchanged from the previous factorial. Independent phase seeds avoid reuse of the radius generator stream. The legacy folded gate uses the unchanged Gate.fit algorithm on4,096 IID latent inputs and their antipodes at the primary budget, or32,768 at the sensitivity budget. It receives the same latent-coordinate information.

All calibrated tables and raw orbit logits are committed before test data or risks are generated. Every limited policy uses exactly64 second evaluations in each batch of256, with independent fixed tie keys shared across methods and policies. Confidence-plus-pi/global/radial masks must be identical. Only selected inputs are evaluated a second time. Raw and full-pi/full-radial policies are distinct-budget references, not universal risk ceilings.

Each trained model receives65,536 independent IID test points, shared across policies and the two calibration budgets; independent Bernoulli draws supply sampled labels. Conditional expected risk and sampled-label risk are both retained. The primary contrast is confidence_radial minus confidence_pi for pullback at8,192, separately in each geometry. Negative means improvement. Bootstrap20,000 complete16-seed clusters within geometry, never treating batches or orbit nodes as independent models. Report95% pointwise intervals for all contrasts and97.5% intervals for the two primary geometry contrasts (Bonferroni family95%). Preserve every failure, seed and losing policy.

Deployment timing includes latent-coordinate conversion, routing, angle computation, rotation and both actual network calls. Randomized policy order, one warm-up and seven retained repeats each process16 real batches of256. Report local CPU timings without accelerator-throughput claims. Calibration cost is separate. Model-evaluation break-even versus universal half-turn counts calibration forwards and the0.75 saved deployment forwards per input. Wall-clock break-even exists only when measured limited-policy latency is lower than the full-pi baseline; otherwise report no finite threshold. Relative calibration overhead targets are10% and1% of cumulative deployment cost. Confidence-plus-pi already uses the same1.25-call deployment budget, so learned angles have no evaluation-count saving over that comparator; their value must come from risk reduction.

Do not infer a causal geometry effect from raw risk differences between separately seeded cohorts. The ellipse is one additional known-symmetry setting, not a test of unknown group discovery or arbitrary distribution shift. No new architecture, schedule, quota, binning, candidate angle or shrinkage tuning is permitted after scoring.
