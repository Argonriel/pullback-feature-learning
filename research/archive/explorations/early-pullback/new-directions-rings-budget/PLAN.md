# Noisy rings under a measured 0.10-second training budget

This follow-up examines the optimizer switch after exploring two moons and concentric rings. Compare pure GD with 100 pullback updates followed by GD. Use the original concentric-rings generator, fixed readout, 80-parameter model, float64 and a single BLAS thread. A pullback update uses rate 8 and damping 0.1. If 100 updates do not fit within the budget, score the valid pullback prefix.

Give each trajectory exactly 100,000,000 nanoseconds of cumulative measured core-update budget. Time the gradient or pullback computation and the full parameter update with a monotonic wall clock. Data generation, warm-up, validation scoring, timing bookkeeping and finite checks stay outside this budget. Retain the last whole update at or before the budget and record both bounding times. Warm the two numerical routines first on separate unscored inputs. Run sequentially. Other OS work can affect wall-clock measurements.

For each method independently search the GD-phase rates 0.03125, 0.125, 0.5, 2, and 8, with one budgeted run for every candidate on each of four tuning pairs. Pair i uses training seed 81000+i, initialization seed 82000+i and validation seed 83000+i, where i = 1,...,4. Search all five rates even when inaccurate or unstable. Choose the lowest mean validation logistic loss over all four pairs, breaking exact ties in favor of the smaller rate. A candidate with any nonfinite trajectory is ineligible. Save all runs, rankings and choices before generating the fresh evaluation arrays.

Use eight disjoint evaluation pairs with training seed 84000+i, initialization seed 85000+i, and validation seed 86000+i. Perform exactly three budgeted runs per method per pair. Interleave pairs and balance first-versus-second method order across evaluation blocks with a fixed schedule RNG seed. For each method average the repeats within each seed first, then report the mean and sample SD across the eight seed means. The primary endpoint is validation logistic loss, the secondary endpoint is fixed-readout validation accuracy. Report the count of paired wins, ties and losses. If any method fails on a fresh pair, retain that record and report incomplete coverage rather than averaging survivors. This assesses two schedules on one machine and varies only the GD-phase learning rate. Do not extend training, change the switch point or revise the grid in response to the results.
