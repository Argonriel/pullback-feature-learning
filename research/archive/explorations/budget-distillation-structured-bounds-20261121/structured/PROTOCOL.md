## Fixed scientific question and primary criterion

Evaluate whether a transform-specific orbit-density bound can certify positive fixed-policy cross-entropy gain where the old global-range TV bound fails, conditional on the explicit (1+a cos phi) family. The primary comparison is known-a and estimated-a versus old global TV at a=.01, epsilon=0, using 2048 batches: report all 16 policy-model lines, positive counts and range/mean of lower bounds. A successful primary result means at least one positive new lower bound while its paired old bound is nonpositive; stronger counts are descriptive, not another test. Always report every law, epsilon, look, model and policy, including failures. The parameter CI extension is a separately displayed conservative version of the same family assumption, not a test that the family is true.

Standard errors are descriptive Monte Carlo precision, not extra familywise certificates or model-population intervals.

For every epsilon, retain three fixed posterior worlds: (i) cosine p=p0+min(epsilon/2,p0,1-p0)cos phi and opposite partner; (ii) adversarial replacing cos phi by sign(d); (iii) orbit_worst with pbar=.5+.5(1-epsilon)sign(cos phi*d), p-pT=epsilon sign(d). The third attains the sharp un-clipped local lower bound for nonnegative a; it is model-dependent. Check posterior range, pointwise TV, correct partner relation and exact orbit identity. Check all corrected gains against the local theorem, with equality in the worst world before Jensen clipping. This supplies genuine negative as well as positive truth outcomes where present; no world is dropped after results.

Algebra audit uses seed 2339000, K={2,3,10}, scales {.1,1,5,20}, 4096 records per cell. Check exact density identity, sharp posterior inequality, constructing extremal posterior equality, common-logit-offset invariance and deterministic ranges. This is a multiclass algebra audit, not a trained multiclass experiment. A fixed 1,048,576-point angular quadrature illustrates the analytic family-misspecification counterexample from THEORY.md: b=.6 third harmonic, h=.2 cos 3phi, invariant p=1, input-independent top-quarter quota.
