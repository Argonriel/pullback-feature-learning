# Reproducibility and evidence map

All values are deterministic small-matrix calculations. There is no statistical
claim, population test, model-performance benchmark, or new training cohort.

## Main candidates

| Folder | Contents | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `universality/` | theorem, primary search ledger, independent review | General kernel proof and fixed-weight coefficients |
| `universality/validation/` | `results.json`, `response_matrices.npz` | Sixteen target/metric contrasts at two quadrature orders; separate mixed-derivative checks; four positive quadrature moment rules |
| `nonisotropic/` | report and primary search ledger | Generic bracket-rank proof and static-loss consequence |
| `nonisotropic/validation_v1/` | `results.json` | Exact curvature, operator derivative, finite differences, one 3x3 rank example at three small coherences, fixed-weight endpoints and two limiting controls |
| `nonisotropic/bures_refined/` | `results.json` | Preserved independent refinement of the pure-Bures control; does not replace its initial output |
| `global_convergence/` | theorem and independent review | Complete global existence/convergence/positivity argument |
| `global_convergence/validation/` | `results.json`, `matrices.npz` | One large-target case, two loss weights and two RK4 steps, plus independently solved endpoint invariants |
| `robustness/GLOBAL_CONVERGENCE_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md` | original-theorem comparisons | Separate novelty review of the global candidate |

The universal-kernel contrasts are derivatives at damping zero, not finite-damping
trajectories. Their direct mixed-derivative comparisons are recorded separately
from the aggregate pass flag. The flag checks the endpoint scaling orders,
quadrature refinement, and positive moment rules; the theorem report separately
states the decreasing truncation discrepancies of the mixed-derivative check.

The nonisotropic endpoint experiment uses full fixed SPD quadratic forms, not just
two scalar learning rates. Bracket generation covers a larger family of controls;
it is not itself evidence that all hidden endpoints are reachable by fixed weights.
The static-loss witness has its own proof and numerical validation.

The global check has a target displacement large enough that its unconstrained
Euclidean affine interpolant is indefinite. The predicted damped endpoint is found
from central invariant equations, independently of integrating the trajectory.
Minimum eigenvalues and invariant extrema in this integration are sampled, not
continuous-time certificates. Convergence for all targets is established by the
proof, not by the finite integration horizon.

## Uncounted lanes and preserved attempts

- `discrete/`: supplementary exact finite-step calculations, including 18
  80-digit converged recurrences, independent jets, factor-Jacobian checks, and an
  exact rational quartic coefficient. `FAILED_ATTEMPTS.md` and
  `failed_initial_source.py` preserve an initial missing-library failure; the final
  implementation uses standard-library Decimal and no package installation.
- `robustness/`: 18 initial variational probes and five focused boundary probes,
  the rejection report, and source comparisons. The results do not constitute a
  proved boundary limit or a retained robustness innovation.

## Source snapshots and provenance

`source_snapshot/experiments/three_candidate_exploration_20261121/` contains all
new reusable source and the usage README, excluding generated bytecode. The global
check's unchanged dependency is included under
`source_snapshot/experiments/endpoint_classification_audit_20261121/` so its relative
import remains valid. The retained failed discrete source is also in its original
research-record location.

`provenance.json` records Python/NumPy versions, source locations, and resolution
of all source/dependency hashes appearing in the JSON records. The results that
do not embed a source hash are tied to their preserved source through the package
manifest; this is not represented as stronger runtime provenance than they have.

`manifest.json` hashes every content file. `ARCHIVE_CHECK.json` records the package
verification, local report-link checks, numerical sanity assertions, and checks
that all manifest-listed files in the five preceding archives are unchanged.
The manifest and archive check are bookkeeping files and do not hash themselves.

No earlier outputs were rewritten. No numerical failure, dependency failure, or
control refinement was silently discarded. A successful integrity check is not a
formal proof certificate or an exhaustive novelty certificate.
