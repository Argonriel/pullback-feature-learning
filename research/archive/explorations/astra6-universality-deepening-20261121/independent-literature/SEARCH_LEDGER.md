## Primary retrieval and scope

The nine originals and inspected theorem locations are listed in `PRIORITY_AUDIT.md`. Byte hashes and retrieval URLs are preserved in `SOURCE_HASHES.json` and `EXTRA_SOURCE_HASHES.json`. Search-engine crawl dates were not treated as publication dates. The reports cite publication metadata in the original paper or publisher entry, not search snippets.

The original Nagaoka–Fujiwara v3 HTML, not an inferred theorem from its abstract, was checked for Theorem 8.6 and Proposition 8.7. The new closest algebraic information-geometry comparison was the original Ohara–Ishi–Tsuchiya publisher article, Theorems 1–3. A second newly checked framework was Thanwerdas–Pennec's smooth kernel-metric definition and full-manifold balanced-metric theorems. The two are relevant prior results, but an explicit separating example or mismatch of quantifiers blocks treating either as the claimed normal-subspace equivalence.

## Failed reductions and excluded novelty claims

1. Full BKM duality uniqueness does not imply normal-subspace rigidity: the latter is vacuous for S=Sym(n).
2. Double autoparallelity at sI requires Jordan-subalgebra closure. Traceless Sym(2) separates this from associator-ideal closure.
3. Associator ideal literature identifies the object. It does not, merely by definition, prove that the two generator tests extend to all of J.
4. Pure SLD integrability and arbitrary control brackets are not equivalent to an endpoint contrast obtainable with one fixed SPD quadratic loss per run.
5. Smooth spectral-kernel metrics already exist. Dropping analyticity must add a genuine rigidity/exception result to carry value.
6. Gaussian moment matching, its maximal degree, uniqueness, and leading error are classical. The moment-to-endpoint translation is the only specialized residual here.
7. No direct equivalent was found for the no-zero-germ theorem in this bounded search. This does not prove none exists under different terminology or as an unpublished/overlooked corollary.
