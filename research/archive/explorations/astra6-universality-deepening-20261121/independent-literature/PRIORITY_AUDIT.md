## 1. Claim audited and comparison criterion

Write E=Sym(n), S a real linear subspace, N=S-perp under the Frobenius inner product, and J=Jordan(S,I). At X0=sI, compare:

1. Local exactness of the N-valued one-form P_N M_X dX restricted to sI+S.
2. The cubic and quartic generator conditions ad_Z^2 U in S and (L_Z+R_Z)ad_Z^2 U in S for every Z,U in S.
3. S is an associator ideal of J.
4. Independence, for every sufficiently small target, of the first damping derivative of the endpoint over fixed SPD quadratic loss weights.

The analytic version excludes the BKM logarithmic kernel. The proposed strengthened version replaces global analyticity with a local nonzero-germ condition on the difference from BKM, while treating local equality with BKM separately. Full Hessian geometry, arbitrary control-field integrability, and doubly autoparallelity are related but different statements. A theorem in any of those areas is not an exact antecedent unless its assumptions and conclusion imply the four statements above with the stated restricted quantifiers.

## 3. Adversarial assessment of the nonanalytic strengthening

The proposed strengthening is meaningful if it proves the following distinction rather than merely replacing a hypothesis: kernels whose difference from BKM is nonzero in every neighborhood of v=0 obey the same exact algebraic classification even if every Taylor coefficient vanishes; kernels that agree with BKM in a neighborhood have locally neutral behavior on every S. The latter shows why global inequality m!=ell is insufficient without analyticity. It also identifies a precise local exception rather than claiming BKM is globally the only exception in the smooth class.

The inspected quantum monotonicity results already imply analyticity for their kernel class. Consequently a smooth flat perturbation is outside that class unless it is zero; such an example must not be called a monotone quantum metric merely because it is pointwise positive. The larger positive spectral class is nevertheless a legitimate Riemannian class, as the general-kernel literature makes clear.

I found no matching no-zero-germ theorem in the inspected originals or targeted search results. This is a negative search observation, not an originality proof. The finite spectral separation argument, the local-exactness reduction, and injectivity of the fixed-weight radial response are distinct proof obligations. Their combination could justify a strengthened candidate if independently proved. The first two are not supplied by the cited quantum metric or Jordan ideal classifications; the last is not supplied merely by controllability or Frobenius integrability.

A publishable account should define closedness as local exactness or path independence if only continuity is assumed; classical exterior differentiation requires more regularity. Flow claims must separately assume enough regularity for existence, uniqueness, parameter differentiation, and the convergent endpoint response. No weak-regularity theorem about one-forms should silently import all these dynamical properties.

## 4. Supporting result and innovation boundary

`MOMENT_CEILING.md` proves the sharp support-count ceiling: an at-most-m-atom positive resolvent mixture cannot match BKM moments through degree 2m, Gaussian matching through 2m-1 is unique, and the first Gaussian error is

    h_m = 2^(4m) / ((4m+1) binom(4m,2m)^2).

This gives an endpoint-order ceiling 4m+1 or 4m+2 only after using the fixed-weight theorem. The exact constant is checked with rational polynomial arithmetic in `moment_constant_check.json`; the maximum discrepancy against the preserved earlier floating-point records is below 3.7e-16. The finite checks are not a proof of the all-m theorem.

The supporting moment result is elementary and useful for delimiting identifiability, but it should not be counted as a separate discovery. Similarly, a claim that all finite diagnostics fail would be false in the intended sense: the algebraic recognition criterion is finite, and a bound on mixture support restores a finite Taylor-order ceiling. The valid limitation concerns a uniformly bounded target-amplitude jet of the first damping endpoint-weight response over the unrestricted metric family.

## 5. Final judgment

Retain and narrow. The strongest potential contribution is a theorem connecting exact normal rigidity, its precise local BKM exception, and fixed-weight endpoint sensitivity without an analyticity assumption, if the full regularity and injectivity details pass independent proof review. The analytic all-k statement is a valid specialized predecessor, not a new Jordan classification. The quadrature family and support ceiling are supporting known mathematics. No priority certification, broad diagnostic impossibility, neural generalization benefit, or global convergence for all spectral kernels is justified by this audit.
