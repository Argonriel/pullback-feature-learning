# Three-candidate exploration register

Opened November 21, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles), after the endpoint classification audit.

User request: continue exploring any potentially innovative direction and report only after at least three candidates have been found. The count refers to independently meaningful questions supported by derivation and minimal validation, not three examples of the same effect. A candidate is not a priority certificate or a demonstrated learning advantage.

Initial lanes: nonisotropic initial states; discrete updates; quantitative robustness; broader spectral metrics. The robustness lane was allowed to pivot when its initial estimates proved standard. A global convergence lane emerged from the previous theorem's unresolved boundary/convergence qualifications.

For each counted candidate require: a precise falsifiable statement; proof or a bounded derivation identifying remaining gaps; a primary-source comparison with attempted direct reductions; a targeted numerical or exact algebraic check; clear hypotheses; and an explicit novelty assessment. Do not count generic Euler drift, generic continuity, scalar-depth mirror potentials, ordinary conservation laws, or the previously identified Jordan class itself.

No retired empirical gate is reopened, no reserved cohort is used, and no external messages are authorized. Prior reports and runs remain immutable. New research records are staged here for a separate fresh archive; reusable source remains in the repository, in English.
