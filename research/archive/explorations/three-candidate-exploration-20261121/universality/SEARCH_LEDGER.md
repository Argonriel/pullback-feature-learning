## Primary texts inspected

1. [Lesniewski and Ruskai, math-ph/9808016](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/9808016). Relevant full text inspected: section 2.3, equations 36-40, Theorems 2.8-2.13, and the BKM/resolvent examples in section 2.5. This supplies the normalized kernel family, integral representation, and standard monotonicity background. The general family and its extremal kernels are known.
2. [Grasselli and Streater, original author PDF](https://math.mcmaster.ca/~grasselli/GrasselliStreater01.pdf). Relevant full text inspected: mixture/exponential connections, sections 3-4, and the full-state BKM uniqueness statement. The theorem is about duality for prescribed full-state connections; it does not classify arbitrary S by a normal cross-block condition.
3. [Fawzi, Saunderson, and Parrilo, 1705.00812](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.00812). Relevant full text inspected: sections 2-3, rational integral approximations and Gaussian quadrature. This occupies the approximation construction. The optimizer obstruction-order consequence is the candidate, not a new quadrature rule.
4. The previous round's original Jordan papers and Nagaoka-Fujiwara/Ciaglia/Marcotte comparisons remain relevant. Their details are preserved in `../robustness/SEARCH_LEDGER.md`, `../nonisotropic/SEARCH_LEDGER.md`, and the previous endpoint-classification archive. No claim is made that every reference in those papers was retrieved or read.

## Failed direct reductions

- Full-state BKM uniqueness does not imply the proposed normal-subspace classification: taking S=Sym(n) makes the latter condition vacuous for every kernel.
- Metric spectral representation alone does not show that arbitrary finite powers of ad_Z recognize the same invariant subspace. The finite spectral-calculus and next-order homogeneity steps are necessary.
- Positive Gaussian quadrature constructs high-order kernel approximants, but does not by itself connect the first unmatched moment to fixed positive quadratic-loss endpoint responses.
- A finite collection of small numerical coefficients cannot establish the arbitrarily delayed family. That statement uses exact quadrature moments and analyticity.

The query coverage is bounded. An equivalent theorem under different terminology or an elementary prior corollary could still exist. The result is retained as a precise candidate with proof and validation, not certified priority.
