# Structural-condition screening protocol

Date: November 21, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). This new analytical screening round follows the completed learned-geometry gate; it does not amend, rerun, rescue, or re-label that failed empirical gate.

The objective is to determine whether a concrete architecture/data condition can predict the sign of adaptive versus frozen-initial-metric risk differences beyond a scalar training clock, or whether the immediate proposed conditions collapse to existing mathematics. No positive finding is assumed. Previous retired joint-asymmetry and readout-decoupling claims stay retired.

Four bounded analytical screens are declared before the new computations:

1. Radial label parity and small residual-output initialization: establish the actual odd/even tangent blocks, then test whether their scaling supplies anything beyond initial spectral filtering and ordinary symmetry breaking.
2. Curvature/readout coupling on an exact low-parameter restriction of the existing two-block tanh model: test whether local non-clock sign or curvature monotonicity controls finite-progress risk sign.
3. Label-replica noise at initialization: derive the exact expected unnormalized metric-feedback acceleration, audit the matched-loss normalization, and test whether a scalar noise/variability statistic can determine benefit/harm without population alignment.
4. Commuting learned metrics: test whether changing eigenvectors is necessary for non-clock predictive differences; distinguish spectral magnitude, trajectory direction, and out-of-sample sign.

Allowed computation is symbolic algebra, finite label enumeration, deterministic derivative checks, and small deterministic low-parameter ODE witnesses. An exact slice holds non-selected parameters fixed and does not represent full-80-parameter training. Examples found by bounded deterministic search must retain the full search space/results and be labeled counterexample search, never a confirmatory performance study. No new broad task sweep, independent confirmation-cohort consumption, population-forecast repackaging, or computational superiority claim is authorized by these screens.

Each screen must state a falsifiable proposition, assumptions, derivation, a reduction or counterexample attempt, numerical checks when needed, nearest original-source overlap, and a keep/reject decision. Stop a proposed universal condition at a valid counterexample. Stop a proposed innovation when it is an inverse/Taylor identity, fixed-feature calculation, elementary parity decomposition, standard stability theorem, generic sample splitting, or a scalar clock change.

If a claim survives, it must specify early available information and a nonroutine finite-progress prediction. It then receives independent mathematical review before any new empirical protocol is proposed. If no claim survives, the deliverable is a completed rejection ledger and the precise unresolved gap; no replacement innovation will be manufactured.

Code and reusable inputs go into the repository's new `experiments/geometry_condition_screen_20261121/` directory. English reports, search records, numerical data, provenance and integrity checks go into a new independent research archive directory. All old artifacts and repository junctions are preserved. No external messages are sent.
