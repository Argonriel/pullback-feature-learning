# Frozen lower-cost verification protocol

The unit of concentration is an independent batch of 256 IID inputs. Each frozen input-only symmetric policy selects exactly 64. Verification uses the interpolated Jensen selector and the strongest histogram resolution selected by the method development protocol. No fresh verification labels, posteriors, risks, or logits choose policies, clipping levels, budgets, or thresholds.

There are 48 fresh models: 16 rings, 16 one-amplitude waveform, and 16 two-amplitude models. All have independent verification input streams. For each model, the primary report uses 512 batches. Nested prefixes 128, 512, 2048 form a simultaneously valid cost curve. The total one-sided error budget .05 is divided among 48 models, three statements (two absolute gains and candidate-minus-folded), and three looks: delta=.05/432 per bound. No certification claim is made at other stopping times.

Absolute gain uses a clipped Jensen gap with c=.25 for rings and c=.5 for each compact waveform task. Clipping choices were made from the old 32-model pilot before fresh states existed: maximize the mean 512-batch lower bound across both old symmetric policies within each old cohort. The new two-amplitude task inherits c=.5 without tuning. All six explored clipping values and all failures remain in development_old32. The pilot's original delta=.05/288 is recorded; fresh confirmation uses the larger 432-event family. Pilot confidence rows are development diagnostics, not simultaneous formal claims across the six explored clips.

The old pilot does not support reducing the primary budget to 128. Recalculation with the final 432-event allocation gives only 1/16 positive bounds for each rings policy; compact one-amplitude positives are 12/16 and 13/16. Mean 128-batch lower-bound retention relative to 2048 is negative on rings and 28%/40% on the waveform task. At 512, all old bounds remain positive; retention is 66% on rings and 85%/88% on waveform. Thus 512 is fixed before fresh access. The original 288-event exploratory rows and the final-family recalculation are both preserved.

Signed relative gain is not clipped. On rings, retain only contributions with norm<=3 in the bounded statistic and subtract the deterministic Gaussian-ring tail allowance in LITERATURE_AND_PROOF.md. Selection still sees the complete original batch. Compact tasks use their full supports. The deterministic gap bound is logcosh(D/2), with D=min(R+C,RL) and R respectively3,1,1.4. The paired batch range width is twice the absolute range, Jmax/2. Relative certificates may remain nonpositive, and no extra sampling or changed bound will follow that result.

The implementation computes both masks from coordinates and independent tie keys, verifies unchanged masks under T, and evaluates both views only on the selected union. All network calls are counted. Standalone absolute verification needs exactly 128 forwards/batch; the joint two-policy run needs at most 256. Actual union costs and wall time are reported separately from calibration, training, coordinate draws, and deployment. The verification source has no posterior or label access.

Runtime measurements cover the actual full 2048-batch shared verification run, including mask audits and bookkeeping; they are descriptive offline timings, not an isolated serving benchmark. Prefix forward counts are exact, but prefix elapsed time is not inferred by dividing total runtime. A joint run is charged once when several bounds reuse the same computed gaps.

Inputs use fresh bases 1121000 (rings), 1123000 (waveform), and 1151000 (two invariants). Tie keys use bases 1122000, 1124000, and 1152000 respectively. Add the model index 1 through 16. Each stream contains 524,288 IID inputs, forming 2048 batches. The full run preserves one batch statistic per policy/clip, signed comparison, quota, union call counts, and hashes needed to regenerate the inputs. Prefix costs are computed from the actual batch ledger. Fresh work requires root approval and a complete frozen policy commit. The old development inputs used bases 1101000 and 1102000, with tie/action offsets 100/200; these were flagged before fresh freeze to prevent reuse as new training streams.

Report positive-bound counts, mean and range of lower bounds, actual forwards/time, first tested positive budget or unreached, and lower-bound retention relative to the 2048-batch lower bound when that denominator is positive. Also report the lower bound as a fraction of the full 2048-batch empirical unclipped gain when positive, explicitly as a descriptive same-stream comparison, not an oracle ratio or an independently certified recovery fraction. Structural zero lower bounds may be stated separately; raw statistical negative bounds are never deleted.

All earlier studies remain unchanged. This pipeline replaces the previous asymmetric allocation rule with a structurally symmetric rule. It removes CDF and interval-bound construction for that new policy class; it is not an acceleration of the same asymmetric certificate. No classifier-accuracy guarantee follows from the logistic-risk result.
